About Luci.

Our Story

Designing with heart since 2012.

Luci began in Addis Ababa with a shared love of honest materials and unhurried rooms. Today we are a team of designers, architects and makers working across homes, hotels and workplaces throughout Ethiopia.

We believe a space should never be decoration alone — it should support the way you sleep, host, think and gather. Every line we draw starts from that idea.

FAQ

Common questions.

How long does a typical project take?

A single room usually takes 6–8 weeks, a full home 4–8 months depending on scale and renovation work.

Do you work outside Addis Ababa?

Yes. We have delivered projects in Bishoftu, Hawassa, Bahir Dar and beyond, and also design remotely through video calls and shared boards.

Do you work with existing furniture?

Absolutely. We love blending treasured pieces with new ones to make a space feel collected over time.

How do you charge for design?

We offer fixed-fee packages in Ethiopian Birr for design and a transparent percentage for project management — no hidden extras.
